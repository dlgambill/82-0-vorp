Who Am I?

MLB mystery star · six clues
6

Six clues. One player. How fast can you name him?

  1. 1The mystery player is always a star: a Hall of Famer, a perennial All-Star or a big-number career. Clue one gives his position and exact years.
  2. 2Type a name and tap him in the list. A wrong guess unlocks the next clue.
  3. 3Next come his honors, his best season, his career numbers, his hometown and finally his teams. Miss on the sixth and the answer is revealed.

There is one new player every day, the same for everyone, plus as many random ones as you want.

    How it works

    Who can be the mystery player. About 540 well-known players who debuted in 1920 or later: Hall of Famers, five-time All-Stars, and anyone with 2,000 hits, 300 home runs, 180 wins, 2,200 strikeouts or 300 saves.

    Who you can guess. About 5,000 players with a real career, so the name list does not give the answer away.

    Where the clues come from. Career records through the 2025 season. Awards counted are MVP, Cy Young, Gold Glove, Silver Slugger and Rookie of the Year. League-leading seasons count home runs, RBI, hits and steals for hitters, and wins, strikeouts and saves for pitchers. A hitter's best season is the one with his most total bases. Team names are the ones used in the years he played there.
