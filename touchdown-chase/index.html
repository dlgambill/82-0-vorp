Touchdown Chase

NFL running backs · career touchdowns
1,500

20 teams. 20 running backs. 1,500 touchdowns.

  1. 1You get a random NFL team.
  2. 2Pick any running back who ever played for them. You see names, not numbers.
  3. 3His career touchdowns go on your total, including the ones he scored for other teams.

Every round is a fresh random draw, so the same team can come up twice. No rerolls, and each back can only be used once. A star who made a late-career stop somewhere still brings his whole career with him.

What counts

Touchdowns are rushing plus receiving, regular season only, through week 11 of the 2026 season. Return and passing touchdowns are not counted.

Who is in the pool. Running backs and fullbacks whose careers started in 1970 or later, about 2,100 of them. Backs who were already playing in the 1960s (Jim Brown, O.J. Simpson, Larry Csonka) are left out because the free data starts in 1970 and their totals would be wrong.

Teams follow the franchise. The Titans include the Houston Oilers, the Raiders include Oakland and Los Angeles, and so on. Years next to a name are his first and last season with that team.

Known gaps. Before 1999, a back traded mid-season is only listed under the teams he also played for in another year.
