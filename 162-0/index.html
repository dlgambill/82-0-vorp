162–0 WAR

MLB roster builder · wins decided by WAR
162–0

Draft nine hitters and five starters. Play 162. Lose none.

  1. 1You get a random team and decade, like the 1920s Yankees.
  2. 2Pick one player from it for an open spot: eight fielders, a DH and five starting pitchers.
  3. 3After 14 picks, the full season plays out game by game.

You get one team reroll and one decade reroll per game. The number next to each player is his WAR in his best season there, and a higher total means more wins. Any hitter can be your DH.

    How wins are decided

    Each player counts for his best WAR season with that franchise in that decade, 1901 through 2025, scaled to a 162-game schedule. Hitters use batting and fielding WAR. Pitchers use pitching WAR and need 15 starts in the season to qualify.

    Team WAR becomes run differential. A team of replacement players wins about 48 games. Every point of WAR adds a win, and a win is worth about 10 runs over a season. The other 12 spots on your roster are replacement level.

    Then 162 games get played. Each game adds the opponent's strength, home field and game-to-game swing. Single games here swing less than in real baseball. With real swings, even the best lineup ever drafted would still lose a dozen.

    Positions. A player can go anywhere he played at least 20 games in a season for that team in that decade. Before outfield spots were tracked separately, outfielders can play all three.
