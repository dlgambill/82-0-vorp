82–0 VORP

NBA roster builder · wins decided by VORP
82–0

Draft five players. Play 82 games. Lose none.

  1. 1You get a random team and decade, like the 1990s Bulls.
  2. 2Pick one player from it and put him at PG, SG, SF, PF or C.
  3. 3After five picks, the full season plays out game by game.

You get one team reroll and one decade reroll per game. The number next to each player is his VORP, and a higher total means more wins.

    How wins are decided

    Each player counts for his best VORP season with that franchise in that decade (Basketball-Reference, through 2025-26). Short seasons are scaled to 82 games.

    Before 1973-74 there is no VORP, so those seasons (1949-50 on) use an estimate from Win Shares: VORP = 0.471 x Win Shares - 0.85. On seasons that have both stats the estimate is typically within 0.6. Estimates are marked with ~.

    Positions. Solid tags are where he was listed with that team. Dashed tags are secondary spots: spots where he played at least 20% of his minutes for that team in a season (tracked since 1996-97). Before 2000, it is any position he was listed at in his career. No penalty for using one.

    Team VORP becomes a net rating. A team of replacement players is about 10 points worse than average per 100 possessions, and every point of VORP buys one back. Your bench is replacement level, so net rating = team VORP minus 10.

    Then 82 games get played. Each night adds the opponent's strength, home court (2.5 points) and normal game-to-game swing. A +10 team wins about 8 of 10. Going 82-0 needs roughly 38 VORP or better, plus luck.
